Khema

เขมา

Private pool villas in a Khmer-style garden, Pattaya. Reopening after renovation.

Khema is an old Pali word for safety, and for peace.

Twenty years of shade

Khema was built twenty years ago in the manner of a Khmer temple garden: carved teak and stone, guardians at the paths, roofs of thatch. Since then the trees have grown up through it and the carvings have darkened. We are keeping all of that.

The villas and pools are being rebuilt now. The garden stays as it is.

  • ShadeShade all day, from trees that grew up with the garden.
  • TouchCarved teak and stone, cool under the hand.
  • SoundLeaves moving overhead.
  • LightLanterns in the branches after dark.

In the morning the courtyard is all shade and moving light.

When the lanterns are lit

After dark the garden turns gold and quiet. Woven lanterns hang in the branches, and the carved gates hold the light.

The villas

Each villa is being rebuilt with its own pool, behind carved doors and garden walls. We will show them here when they are finished, not before.
